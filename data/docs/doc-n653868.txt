NIBCO N653868
NIBCO Bronze Coupling, 1-1/4 in, Solder x Solder, 150 psi

Overview
A cast bronze coupling for potable water, hydronic heating and low-pressure steam service, machined to ASME B16.18 socket dimensions so it seats squarely on Type K, L and M copper tube.

Features
- Cast bronze body for corrosion resistance in potable water
- Solder x solder socket ends to ASME B16.18
- Lead-free composition compliant with NSF/ANSI 61 and 372
- Suitable for hydronic heating and low-pressure steam
- Full socket depth for a reliable capillary joint

Applications
- Potable water distribution
- Hydronic heating

Specifications
Nominal Size: 1-1/4 in
End Connection: Solder x Solder
Material Construction: Cast Bronze
Pressure Rating: 150 psi
Maximum Temperature: 250 deg F
Standard: ASME B16.18, NSF/ANSI 61
Finish: Mill
Lead Free: Yes
Package Quantity: 1 ea
