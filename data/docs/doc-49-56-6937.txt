MILWAUKEE HOLE DOZER Bi-Metal Hole Saw, 1-1/4 in.

Overview
HOLE DOZER hole saws carry a thick, rigid backing plate and a variable-pitch bi-metal tooth pattern that clears chips fast in wood, metal and plastic without binding.

Features
- Bi-metal construction for long life in metal
- Rip guard teeth resist tooth strippage
- Thick backing plate reduces flex
- Two plug ejection slots make slug removal quick
- Colour-coded size marking for fast identification

Applications
- Boring for conduit
- Boring for plumbing penetrations
