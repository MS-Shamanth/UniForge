Grip-Rite GR328782
Grip-Rite PrimeGuard Plus Deck Screw, #8 x 1-5/8 in, Zinc Plated, 5 lb

Overview
A coated deck screw with a Type 17 auger point that starts without pre-drilling and a triple-layer PrimeGuard Plus coating approved for contact with ACQ pressure-treated lumber.

Features
- Type 17 auger point starts without pre-drilling
- PrimeGuard Plus triple-layer coating for ACQ lumber
- Bugle head seats flush without splitting the board
- Square drive recess resists cam-out
- Meets ICC-ES AC257 for corrosion resistance

Applications
- Deck board fastening
- Pressure-treated lumber
