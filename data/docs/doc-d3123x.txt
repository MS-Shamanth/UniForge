Diablo D3123X
Diablo 7-1/4 in. x 24 Tooth Circular Saw Blade for Wood

Overview
The Diablo circular saw blade uses a TiCo hi-density carbide blend and a laser-cut, tensioned steel plate to hold a true line through framing lumber and sheet goods, with a Perma-Shield coating that shrugs off pitch build-up.

Features
- TiCo hi-density carbide teeth for extended edge life
- Laser-cut stabiliser vents cut noise and vibration
- Perma-Shield non-stick coating resists heat, gumming and corrosion
- Tensioned plate keeps the blade true at speed
- Thin kerf reduces load on the saw motor

Applications
- Framing
- Ripping sheet goods

Specifications
Diameter: 7-1/4 in
Tooth Count: 24 T
Arbor Size: 5/8 in
Kerf: 0.071 in
Maximum Speed: 8500 rpm
Tooth Material: TiCo Carbide
Application: Wood, Plywood, OSB
Package Quantity: 1 ea
