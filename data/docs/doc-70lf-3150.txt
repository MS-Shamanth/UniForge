Apollo 70LF-3150
Apollo Bronze Ball Valve, 2 in, Full Port, 600 psi WOG, Lever Handle

Overview
A two-piece bronze ball valve with a full-port chrome-plated brass ball and reinforced PTFE seats, rated for water, oil and gas service, with a blowout-proof stem and a packing gland that stays serviceable in place.

Features
- Full port for minimal pressure drop
- Chrome-plated brass ball and blowout-proof stem
- Reinforced PTFE seats and thrust washer
- Adjustable packing gland serviceable in place
- Vinyl-coated lever handle
- Lead-free bronze body

Applications
- Water shutoff
- Compressed air isolation

Specifications

Port Type: Full Port
Handle Type: Lever
Ball Material: Chrome Plated Brass
Seat Material: Reinforced PTFE
Maximum Temperature: 406 deg F
