Delta DL6550-PC
Delta Lahara Two-Handle 4 in. Centerset Bathroom Faucet, Polished Chrome

Overview
The Delta Lahara lavatory faucet uses a Diamond Seal valve with a diamond-coated ceramic disc, which keeps water off the internal waterway and holds a drip-free seal for the life of the fixture.

Features
- DIAMOND Seal Technology reduces leak points
- InnoFlex PEX waterways keep water inside the faucet
- WaterSense labelled at 1.2 gpm
- Two-handle operation with metal lever handles
- Pop-up drain assembly included

Applications
- Residential bathroom lavatory
