Rheem XE71M72H21
Rheem Performance 40 Gallon Electric Water Heater, 240 V, 4500 W, 6 Year Warranty

Overview
A 40 gallon residential electric water heater with two 4500 watt copper immersion elements, a factory-installed temperature and pressure relief valve, and a premium-grade anode rod for extended tank life in aggressive water.

Features
- Two copper immersion heating elements for fast recovery
- Premium grade anode rod extends tank life
- Factory installed temperature and pressure relief valve
- Automatic thermostat with high-limit control
- Thick non-CFC foam insulation reduces standby loss
- Six year limited tank and parts warranty

Applications
- Residential potable hot water
