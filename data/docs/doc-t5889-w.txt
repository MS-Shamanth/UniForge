Leviton T5889-W
Leviton Tamper-Resistant Duplex Receptacle, 20 A, 125 V, NEMA 5-20R, White

Overview
A commercial-specification tamper-resistant duplex receptacle with a shutter mechanism that blocks foreign objects while accepting a standard plug, and back-and-side wiring for fast, secure terminations.

Features
- Tamper-resistant shutter meets NEC 406.12
- Back and side wire terminations
- Thermoplastic face resists impact and abrasion
- Steel mounting strap with brass-plated contacts
- UL listed and CSA certified

Applications
- Commercial branch circuits
- Residential dwelling units
