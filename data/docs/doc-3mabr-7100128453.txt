3M Cubitron II Fibre Disc 982C, 80+, 7 in x 7/8 in, 25 per carton

Overview
3M Cubitron II Fibre Disc 982C uses electrostatically oriented precision shaped ceramic grain that slices through metal rather than ploughing it, cutting cooler and lasting substantially longer than conventional ceramic discs on carbon steel and stainless.

Features
- Precision Shaped Grain slices through metal for a cooler cut
- Cuts faster with less operator pressure
- Heavy-duty fibre backing for aggressive stock removal
- Performs on carbon steel, stainless steel and aluminium
- Reduces total abrasive cost per part
- 25 discs per carton

Applications
- Weld removal
- Bevelling
- Heavy stock removal
