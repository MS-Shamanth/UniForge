Milwaukee 49-94-6729
6 in. x .045 in. x 5/8 in. Metal Cut-Off Wheel, Type 1, 25 Pack

Overview
The MILWAUKEE Metal Cut-Off Wheel is engineered for fast, burr-free cuts in ferrous metal, stainless steel and threaded rod. Double fibreglass reinforcement resists side-load fracture, and a hardened aluminum oxide grain holds its edge through repeated passes so operators change wheels less often.

Features
- Fast, burr-free cutting in ferrous metals and stainless steel
- Double fibreglass reinforcement resists side-load fracture
- Aluminum oxide grain rated 36/40 balances cut rate against wheel life
- Type 1 flat profile for straight cutting
- Fits 5/8 in. arbor angle grinders and cut-off tools
- Also offered with a 5/8" DKO Arbor for larger grinders
- Maximum operating speed matched to wheel diameter
- Burst tested to ANSI B7.1
- Built for high-cycle production and maintenance work
- Packaged 25 per box for shop use

Applications
- Cutting ferrous metal stock
- Cutting stainless steel
- Cutting threaded rod and rebar
